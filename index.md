I'm an astrophysicist interested in studying the internal structure and possible formation and evolution history of super-Earths and sub-Neptunes, both through observations and modelling.

The main project of my PhD was the development of [the plaNETic framework](https://github.com/joannegger/plaNETic), a neural network-based open-source tool that can be used to rapidly infer the internal structure of observed exoplanets based on their measured radii, masses, and orbital periods. 
The framework has been applied in [a large number of observational publications](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/public-libraries/1gUfHn6dR5qTwZ9phcfNMg), many of which I have actively contributed to.

In addition, I am an external collaborator of the CHEOPS science team, where I lead an observational programme aimed at improving the radius precision of small exoplanets which potentially host a volatile-rich envelope. 
I also have successfully obtained observing time on both HARPS and ESPRESSO and am a member of the PLATO mission consortium.

Since September 2025, I am a research fellow at the European Space Agency, based at ESTEC (Noordwijk, NL). Before that, I completed my PhD at the University of Bern (CH) in May 2025.
